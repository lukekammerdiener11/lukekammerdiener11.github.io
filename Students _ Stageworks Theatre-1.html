What to Wear

Short answer: dress comfortably. There's no formal dress code at Stageworks.

  • Casual is welcome. Jeans and a nice top are perfectly fine.
  • Dressing up? Date night or opening night? Go for it.
  • Bring a layer. Theatres can run chilly.
  • Comfortable shoes for walking from parking.

Theatre Etiquette

A few simple things that make the night better for everyone, including the actors.

  • Arrive 20–30 minutes early to find parking and your seat.
  • Silence your phone. No photos or recording during the show.
  • Food and drink rules: check with the box office.
